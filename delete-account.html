Delete Your Account & Data

Last updated: June 8, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Drink Watch account and the data associated with it. Drink Watch is developed by Akiva Blackman.

Delete your entire account

You can permanently delete your account directly inside the app:

  1. Open Drink Watch and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile button in the top-right of the Home screen to open your Profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete Account."
  4. Confirm. Your account and all associated data are permanently deleted.
🗑️ Can't access the app? Email akivablackman.studios@gmail.com from your account's email address and we'll delete your account and data for you.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove data:

  • Delete a logged drink: on the Home screen, swipe a drink entry and tap delete.
  • Leave a group: open the group and choose Leave Group to remove your membership.

What is deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove:

  • Your account and login (email).
  • Your profile, including your display name and any avatar image you uploaded.
  • All of your drink logs and statistics.
  • Your group memberships, and any groups you own.

What is kept

  • Drink photos: none are kept — photos used for AI identification are never stored in the first place.
  • Purchase records: if you left a tip, the transaction record is held by Apple or Google under their own policies and is outside our control. We do not store your payment details.
  • Legal/financial records: we may retain limited records where required by law.

Retention period

Account deletion takes effect immediately. Any residual copies in our encrypted backups are purged within 30 days.
