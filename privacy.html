Drink Watch

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Drink Watch ("we," "us," or "the app") is a social drink tracker for group events. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We built the app to be privacy-friendly by default: we don't run ads, we don't use analytics or tracking SDKs, and we never sell your data.

📸 Photos you take to identify a drink are never stored. They are sent for identification, processed in memory, and immediately discarded.

Information we collect

  • Account email. We use your email address to sign you in with a "magic link." We do not use passwords.
  • Profile details. A display name and, optionally, a profile avatar you choose.
  • Drink logs. The drinks you record — type, size, estimated standard drinks, calories, sugar, and timestamps.
  • Group data. Groups you create or join, their names and dates, your membership, and invite codes.
  • Drink photos and descriptions. When you use AI identification, the photo or text you provide is sent to our AI provider to identify the drink. Photos are not retained by us or by the provider after identification.
  • Purchases. If you leave an optional tip, the purchase is processed by Apple or Google. We receive confirmation that a purchase occurred, but we never receive or store your payment-card details.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, or device advertising identifier, and we do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

How we use your information

  • To provide the core features: logging drinks, showing your stats, and powering group leaderboards and activity feeds.
  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To identify drinks you photograph or describe.
  • To process optional tips through the app stores.

Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the app:

  • Supabase — our backend provider, which hosts the database and authentication on our behalf.
  • Anthropic — our AI provider, which receives drink photos or text solely to identify a drink. This data is not used to train models and is not retained for this purpose.
  • Apple and Google — process in-app purchases (tips) and provide app distribution.

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Data retention

Your account and drink history are kept until you delete them. You can delete your account at any time from within the app (on your Profile screen), which permanently removes your account and associated data. Drink photos are never retained, as described above.

Your rights

You can access and update your profile in the app, and you can delete your account and data at any time. To make any other request about your data, contact us at the email below.

Children

Drink Watch is intended for adults of legal drinking age and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with our backend provider under access controls. Session tokens are stored securely on your device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
